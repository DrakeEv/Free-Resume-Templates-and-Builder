How to edit and save your resume

Required: Save your resume in Google Drive, using your school account or your personal Gmail. That way it's backed up, and you can update it from any device.

Drive turns your PDF into a document you can type in. This works best on a computer.

  1. 1

    Download your PDF

    Download PDFClassic-Resume.pdf

    Build your template above and download it to your computer.

  2. 2

    Upload it to Drive

    + NewNew folderFile uploadFolder upload

    Go to drive.google.com and sign in with your school account or personal Gmail. Click New, then File upload, and choose your PDF.

  3. 3

    Open with Google Docs

    PreviewOpen with ›Google Docs

    Right-click the file. Choose Open with, then Google Docs.

  4. 4

    Edit and save as PDF

    [Your Name]Maria LopezFile › Download › PDF

    Replace every [bracket] with your info. Your Google Doc saves automatically in Drive. To apply, click File › Download › PDF document.

Good to know: When Docs converts a PDF, some lines, colors or spacing can shift. Check the layout and fix anything that moved before you save.

Before you send your resume

  1. Keep it to one page. Put your strongest experience first.
  2. Start each bullet with an action verb: led, built, organized, trained.
  3. Add numbers where you can, such as hours, people served or money raised.
  4. Replace every [bracketed] placeholder and delete sections you don't need.
  5. Save it as a PDF named Lastname-Firstname-Resume.pdf.
  6. Bring it to Career Services for a free review before you apply.
